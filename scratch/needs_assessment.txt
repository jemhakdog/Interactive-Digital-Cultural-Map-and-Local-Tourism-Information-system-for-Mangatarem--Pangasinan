Current Issues & Challenges
Help us understand the specific inefficiencies your office currently faces.
The LGU Mangatarem Tourism Office faces issues including fragmented and largely manual information 
management, irregularly updated online content, and a lack of standardized or centralized tourism 
materials. These challenges, compounded by limited resources and slow coordination with stakeholders 
who still depend on traditional communication methods, underscore the need to integrate more advanced 
technologies to improve the accuracy, speed, and overall efficiency of disseminating cultural and tourism 
information.
The current method creates challenges because manual processes and traditional communication slow 
down updates, make information harder to organize, and often lead to inconsistent or outdated content. As 
a result, sharing accurate and timely cultural and tourism information becomes more difficult.

System Objectives
The ultimate goal of this system is to make it easier and faster to manage, update, and share accurate 
cultural and tourism information, so that tourists, stakeholders, and the community can access reliable 
information anytime and help promote Mangatarem effectively.
