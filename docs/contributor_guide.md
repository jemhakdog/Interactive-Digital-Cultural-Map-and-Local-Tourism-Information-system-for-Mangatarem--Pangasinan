# Barangay Contributor Guide

Thank you for helping document and preserve the cultural heritage of Mangatarem! As a **Category 1 User** (Administrative and Stakeholder portal), you play a vital role in keeping our system accurate and vibrant at the grassroots level.

## Your Role and Responsibilities

- **Content Creation**: Submit information about attractions, events, and cultural heritage sites.
- **Accuracy**: Ensure that all names, dates, and locations are correct.
- **Photography**: Upload clear, high-quality images of local landmarks.
- **Reporting**: Inform the Tourism Office of any changes or corrections needed for your barangay.

---

## Submitting New Content

### 1. Adding an Attraction
1. Go to **Dashboard > Add Attraction**.
2. Provide a descriptive **Name**.
3. Use the **Map Picker** to set the exact **Latitude** and **Longitude**.
4. Upload at least one **Primary Image**.
5. Click **Submit for Review**.

### 2. Adding an Event
1. Go to **Dashboard > Add Event**.
2. Select the **Event Type** (Religious, Civic, etc.).
3. Choose the **Start** and **End Dates**.
4. Click **Submit**.

---

## Cultural Heritage Registry (Forms 01-07)

This is a specialized section for documenting structured cultural assets.

### 1. Starting a Heritage Profile
1. Navigate to **Heritage Registry > Create New**.
2. Select the **Category** that best fits the asset (e.g., Natural, Built).
3. Fill out the corresponding **Heritage Form** (Forms 01-07).
4. Save as **Draft** if you need to return later, or click **Submit** once complete.

### 2. Form Descriptions
- **Form 01A - Natural Heritage**: For mountains, springs, and land formations.
- **Form 02A - Built Heritage**: For churches, monuments, and historical buildings.
- **Form 03A - Movable Heritage**: For artifacts, tools, and artworks.
- **Form 04A - Intangible Heritage**: For oral traditions, festivals, and music.
- **Form 05 - Personality Profile**: For significant historical or local figures.
- **Form 06 - Cultural Institution**: For libraries, museums, and school organizations.
- **Form 07 - LGU Culture Program**: For government-led cultural initiatives.

---

## Guidelines for Success

- **Be Detailed**: Use full sentences and provide as much historical context as possible.
- **Check Your Spelling**: Names of people and places must be accurate.
- **Photography Matters**: Avoid blurry or low-light photos.
- **Follow the ERD Mapping**: Ensure coordinates are set correctly during the submission process.
