# Contributor Guide

**Interactive Digital Cultural Map and Local Tourism Information System for Mangatarem, Pangasinan**

This guide is for **Barangay Contributors** who submit and manage cultural content for their respective barangays.

---

## Overview

As a **Contributor**, you play a vital role in showcasing your barangay's cultural heritage, tourism attractions, and community events. This guide will help you understand your responsibilities and how to effectively contribute content to the platform.

---

## Contributor Role

### Who Can Be a Contributor?

- **Barangay Representatives**: Official barangay staff or designated cultural officers
- **Community Leaders**: Recognized community leaders approved by the LGU
- **Tourism Coordinators**: Local tourism desk personnel

### What Can Contributors Do?

✅ **Submit Content**:
- Add new attractions in your barangay
- Create event listings for barangay festivals and activities
- Upload photos and videos to the gallery
- Update barangay information and cultural profile

✅ **Manage Submissions**:
- View status of submitted content (pending/approved/rejected)
- Edit pending submissions before review
- Track engagement on approved content

❌ **What Contributors Cannot Do**:
- Approve or reject content (admin-only)
- Manage content from other barangays
- Access system-wide analytics
- Modify approved content after publication

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Account Creation

1. **Register** at the platform's registration page
2. Fill in required information:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Password
   - **Barangay affiliation** (select your barangay)
   - **Role**: Select "Contributor"
3. Submit registration

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Approval Required**: Contributor accounts must be approved by an admin before you can submit content. Contact the LGU Tourism Office if your account is not activated within 24 hours.

### 2. Account Approval

After registration:
- Your account will be in **pending** status
- If you attempt to log in before approval, you will be redirected to a dedicated "Account Pending Review" page explaining the verification process (typically 24-48 hours).
- An LGU admin will review your application
- You'll receive email notification once approved
- Login credentials will work and redirect you to your dashboard after approval

### 3. First Login

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username and password
3. You'll be redirected to the **Barangay Dashboard**

## Submitting Content

### Adding an Attraction

**What qualifies as an attraction?**
- Natural landmarks (springs, caves, mountains, waterfalls)
- Historical sites (churches, monuments, old buildings)
- Religious sites (chapels, shrines, pilgrimage sites)
- Cultural centers or museums
- Local businesses of cultural significance

**Submission Process**:

1. **Navigate**: Barangay Dashboard → **Attractions** → **Add New Attraction**

2. **Fill in Details**:
   - **Name**: Official or commonly known name
   - **Category**: Select appropriate category
     - Nature
     - Historical
     - Religious
     - Cultural
     - Food & Dining
   - **Description**: Detailed description (minimum 100 characters)
     - What makes it special?
     - Historical background (if applicable)
     - Activities available
     - Best time to visit
   - **Location**:
     - Click on the map to set coordinates
     - Or enter latitude/longitude manually
   - **Photo**: Upload a clear, high-quality image (JPG, PNG, max 5MB)

3. **Submit for Review**

**Best Practices**:
- ✅ Use clear, descriptive names
- ✅ Provide comprehensive descriptions
- ✅ Upload high-quality, well-lit photos
- ✅ Ensure accurate location coordinates
- ❌ Avoid duplicate submissions
- ❌ Don't submit private property without permission
- ❌ Avoid blurry or low-quality images

---

### Creating an Event

**What qualifies as an event?**
- Barangay fiestas
- Religious celebrations
- Cultural festivals
- Community activities
- Sports tournaments
- Educational programs

**Submission Process**:

1. **Navigate**: Barangay Dashboard → **Events** → **Add New Event**

2. **Fill in Details**:
   - **Title**: Event name
   - **Description**: What happens during the event?
   - **Date & Time**: When the event occurs
   - **Location**: Where it takes place
   - **Category**: 
     - Religious
     - Civic
     - Entertainment
   - **Image**: Event poster or representative photo

3. **Submit for Review**

**Best Practices**:
- ✅ Submit events at least 2 weeks in advance
- ✅ Include complete schedule information
- ✅ Mention if registration is required
- ✅ Provide contact information for inquiries
- ❌ Don't submit past events
- ❌ Avoid incomplete information

---

### Uploading Gallery Items

**What to upload?**
- Cultural activities and traditions
- Barangay landmarks
- Community events
- Local crafts and products
- Historical photographs

**Upload Process**:

1. **Navigate**: Barangay Dashboard → **Gallery** → **Upload**

2. **Select File**:
   - **Photos**: JPG, PNG (max 5MB)
   - **Videos**: MP4 (max 50MB, under 2 minutes)

3. **Add Details**:
   - **Caption**: Describe what's shown
   - **Category**: Link to attraction or event (optional)

4. **Submit for Review**

**Best Practices**:
- ✅ High resolution (minimum 1920x1080 for photos)
- ✅ Proper lighting and composition
- ✅ Culturally respectful content
- ✅ Obtain consent from people in photos
- ❌ No copyrighted images without permission
- ❌ No offensive or inappropriate content

---

### Updating Barangay Information

Update your barangay's cultural profile:

1. **Navigate**: Barangay Dashboard → **Profile** → **Edit Barangay Info**

2. **Update Sections**:
   - **History**: Historical background of your barangay
   - **Cultural Assets**: Heritage sites, traditional practices
   - **Traditions**: Annual traditions and customs
   - **Local Practices**: Unique practices or industries
   - **Unique Features**: What makes your barangay special?

3. **Save Changes**

## Content Approval Workflow

### Submission Status

All submitted content goes through a review process:

| Status | Description | What You Can Do |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting admin review | Edit or delete submission |
| **Approved** | Publicly visible on the platform | View engagement statistics |
| **Rejected** | Not suitable for publication | View admin feedback, resubmit with changes |

### Review Timeline

- **Target**: 24-48 hours for review
- **Delays**: Higher volume during peak seasons (fiesta period)
- **Notifications**: Email notification when status changes

### If Your Content Is Rejected

1. **Check Admin Feedback**: Review the reason for rejection
2. **Make Corrections**: Address the issues mentioned
3. **Resubmit**: Create a new submission with improvements

**Common Rejection Reasons**:
- Incomplete information
- Low-quality images
- Duplicate content
- Inaccurate location data
- Content outside barangay jurisdiction

## Content Guidelines

### Quality Standards

**Text Content**:
- Clear, grammatically correct English or Filipino
- Minimum description length: 100 characters
- Respectful and culturally sensitive language
- Factually accurate information

**Images**:
- Minimum resolution: 1280x720 pixels
- Clear, well-lit, in-focus
- Representative of the subject
- No watermarks (unless you own the image)

**Videos**:
- Stable, not shaky
- Good audio quality
- Under 2 minutes duration
- Relevant to cultural/tourism theme

### Content to Avoid

❌ **Prohibited Content**:
- Offensive or discriminatory material
- Political campaigning
- Commercial advertising (unless culturally significant)
- Copyrighted content without permission
- Misleading or false information
- Personal attacks or defamation

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Cannot Upload Images

**Possible Causes**:
- File size too large (> 5MB)
- Unsupported format
- Slow internet connection

**Solutions**:
- Compress images before uploading
- Use JPG or PNG format only
- Check your internet connection

### Issue: Location Not Setting Correctly

**Solution**:
- Ensure location services are enabled in your browser
- Manually enter coordinates from Google Maps
- Zoom in on the map before clicking to set location

### Issue: Account Still Pending

**Solution**:
- Check your email for admin response
- Contact LGU Tourism Office directly
- Verify you selected "Contributor" role during registration

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How many attractions can I submit?

**A**: No limit. Submit as many legitimate attractions as exist in your barangay.

### Q: Can I edit approved content?

**A**: No. Once approved, content is locked. Contact an admin if corrections are needed.

### Q: What if my barangay has no tourist attractions?

**A**: Every barangay has unique features! Consider:
- Historical markers or old buildings
- Natural features (rivers, trees, views)
- Cultural traditions or practices
- Local products or crafts
- Community gathering places

### Q: Can I upload videos from events?

**A**: Yes, as long as:
- You have permission to share the video
- Content is appropriate and culturally respectful
- File size is under 50MB
- Duration is under 2 minutes

### Q: How do I add multiple photos to one attraction?

**A**: Upload via the Gallery section and tag them to the attraction after approval.
