**General Guidelines**

- All pages must have BCC Letter head **EXCEPT FOR THE TITLE PAGE(First Page of the Preliminaries)**. You may download the letterhead in this link:  [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1O2W6gjlmMXUbMw-U5Q2756ytoaiq0\_l5/view?usp=sharing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1O2W6gjlmMXUbMw-U5Q2756ytoaiq0_l5/view?usp=sharing)

- Preserve the inverted pyramid text structure on both the cover page and the title page.

**Paper Body Formatting:** 

**The body of the paper (Chapter 1 to 4\) begins on a new page after Preliminary Pages.**

Order of Pages: **Follow the sequence of pages outlined in the Table of Contents in Prelim Template**

Font to be used in all Pages: **Tahoma**

Font Size of all Texts in All Pages: **12  (can be reduced to 10 if necessary)**

Spacing**:  1.5 Spacing**

**Margins in All Pages:**

	Top Margin: **1.5 inch**

	Left Margin: **1.5 inch**

	Right Margin: **1.0 inch**

Bottom Margin: **1.0 inch**

Paragraph Text Alignment:  **Justify**

Paragraph Indention: **0.5 inch or 1 tab from the margin**

**Pagination:**

	Page Number Location: **Bottom, Centered**

	Page Numbering in Preliminaries Pages: **small Roman Numerals (**e.g.  **iv)**

Page Numbering for the rest of Document Pages: **Arabic Numerals (**e.g.  **32 )**
