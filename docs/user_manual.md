# User Manual (Visitor Guide)

Welcome to the **Interactive Digital Cultural Map of Mangatarem**! This guide helps you navigate and explore the town's rich cultural and tourism assets.

## Exploring the Map

The interactive map is the heart of the system.

### 1. Navigating Markers
- **Click any marker** to see a quick summary of the attraction or event.
- **Cluster Markers**: When multiple items are close together, they appear as a numbered circle. Click to zoom in.
- **Directions**: Click the link in the popup to get directions via your device's native map application.

### 2. Filtering Content
- Use the **Category Filter** to switch between Natural, Religious, Historical, or Recreational sites.
- Filter by **Barangay** to explore specific parts of the town.

---

## Heritage Registry Exploration

The system includes a structured **Cultural Heritage Registry** (Forms 01-07).

- **How to access**: Visit the "Registry" tab from the main menu.
- **What to find**: Detailed historical records, significant cultural figures, and intangible traditions like local songs or crafts.

---

## Personalized Features

### 1. Your Favorites
- Click the **Heart icon** on any attraction to save it.
- View your saved items under **Your Profile > Favorites**.

### 2. Event Interests
- Mark yourself as **Interested** or **Going** to local festivals.
- Receive updates or reminders about upcoming activities.

---

## Progressive Web App (PWA)

### 1. Installing the App
- **Android/iOS**: Open the website in your browser and select "Add to Home Screen" from the menu.
- **Desktop**: Click the install icon in the browser address bar.

### 2. Offline Mode
The PWA caches essential map data and basic information, allowing you to view recent content even when your internet connection is weak.
