# Administrative Guide

This guide is for **Admin** (LGU) and **Contributor** (Barangay Representative) roles. It explains how to manage content on the Mangatarem Cultural Map platform.

## Role Overview

- **Admin**: Full access to the system, including user approval, data management across all barangays, and system configuration.
- **Contributor**: Local representatives who can manage attractions and events specifically for their assigned Barangay.

## Content Management

### Managing Attractions
1. **Access Dashboard**: Log in and select "Admin Dashboard".
2. **Add New Attraction**:
   - Provide the name, precise location (captured from the map or entered manually), and a detailed description.
   - Assign a category (e.g., Nature, Historical).
   - Upload high-quality images.
3. **Approve Submissions**: Admins must review user-submitted content before it appears on the public map.

### Events and Festivals
- Use the **Events Manager** to add upcoming festivities.
- Ensure dates and locations are accurate to help visitors plan their trips.

### Gallery Management
- Upload and curate photos and videos that showcase local traditions and practices.

## User Management (Admins Only)

- **Approving Contributors**: Review and approve accounts for barangay representatives.
- **Role Assignment**: Elevate users to contributor status for specific barangays.

## Analytics

- Monitor the **Analytics Dashboard** to see which attractions are most popular.
- Use view data to identify areas with high engagement and potential for tourism development.
