# 🏛️ CBIS Community Stewardship Guide

This guide describes the Community-Based Information System (CBIS) stewardship model and how Barangay Representatives (Stewards) can manage their community's cultural heritage.

## 📍 What is Community Stewardship?

In the Mangatarem Cultural Map, we distinguish between **Ownership** and **Stewardship**:

*   **Ownership**: The individual contributor who first researched and uploaded an asset.
*   **Stewardship**: The collective responsibility of the Barangay Representative to maintain, update, and oversee *all* assets within their barangay.

As a Steward, you have administrative access to all cultural hotspots and events in your jurisdiction, even if you weren't the one who originally created them.

---

## 🛠️ Managing Community Assets

### 1. Unified Dashboard
When you log in to your **Barangay Portal**, your dashboard shows statistics for the **entire barangay**. This includes:
*   Total approved attractions in your community.
*   Upcoming festivals and civic events.
*   Pending submissions from other local contributors.

### 2. The Shared Heritage View
Under the **Hotspots** and **Events** menus, you will see a list of every asset assigned to your barangay. 
*   **View Contributor**: See who originally added the data.
*   **Mass Oversight**: You can edit or suggest updates for any item to ensure accuracy.

---

## 📅 Managing Community Events (PGIS)

The system uses a Participatory GIS (PGIS) approach where your event data is directly fed into the public map.

1.  **Add Coordinates**: When adding an event (e.g., a Patronal Feast), you must include its **Latitude** and **Longitude**.
2.  **Public Sync**: Once approved, events appear on the public map with a 📅 icon.
3.  **Automatic Framing**: The public Barangay Profile will automatically adjust its map view to ensure all your attractions and events are visible in one frame.

---

## 📖 Narrative Stewardship

Your barangay's identity is more than just markers on a map. You are responsible for maintaining the narrative context:

*   **Mission & Vision**: Define the official goals for your community's cultural preservation.
*   **Historical Background**: Ensure the "History" section of your profile reflects official records.

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Settings** menu in your portal to update your Barangay's Mission and Vision statements. These appear prominently on your public profile cards.

---

## 🛡️ Best Practices
*   **Accuracy First**: As a steward, you verify the historical claims of other contributors.
*   **Geo-Precision**: Always double-check Latitude/Longitude to ensure visitors can find your locations.
*   **Attribution**: Respect original researchers by keeping their names associated with the items they documented.
