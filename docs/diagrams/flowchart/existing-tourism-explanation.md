# Explanation: Existing Tourism Flowchart (Manual Navigation)

This flowchart represents the traditional experience of a visitor arriving in Mangatarem before the implementation of the Digital Cultural Map.

### Challenges in the Legacy Process:
1.  **Physical Dependency**: Tourists are forced to physically visit the Municipal Tourism Office to acquire information.
2.  **Fragmented Information**: Data is provided via brochures or verbal directions, which are often incomplete or difficult to retain.
3.  **Manual Navigation (Bottleneck)**: Without a centralized digital map, tourists must rely on primitive paper maps or local inquiries. This is a high-risk step for getting lost or taking inefficient routes.
4.  **Information Gap**: There is no way to check the current status of an attraction (e.g., if it's closed for renovation or fully booked) until the tourist physically arrives at the site.

### Strategic Solution:
The Digital Cultural Map eliminates these hurdles by providing real-time, GPS-enabled navigation and up-to-date attraction statuses accessible directly from any mobile device.
